#glitchrune-character
**Nacht Nullam** (English: \[nɔkt nʌɫ.ɫəm]) is a minor character in ''[[Glitchrune]]'', important to the lore, but dead by the time of the main story. Their present-day counterpart is [[Solin Leihinheim]].
# Plot
Nacht is one of the [[ancient heroes of Deltaverse]], a team of adventurers that were active during, and helped end, the [[Great War of the Worlds]]. They were a toolsmith at heart, and created or helped create most of the ancient weapons of the world of Glitchrune, including the [[Daybreaker]], [[Medina Gutierrez]]'s signature weapon.
# Trivia
## Name origin
Nacht's first name is literally the German word for "night". The last name Nullam is from the Latin word *nulla*, meaning zero.
