## Skill
Lightshow Collection - Support AOE
Gains 6 Punchline. All allies other than Keichitsu receive "Snow Knight" for 3 of Keichitsu's turns (outside of Aha Instant). They receive Shield equal to half of Keichitsu's DEF plus 100. At the beginning of Keichitsu's action (including Aha Instant), those with "Hide the Pain" receive Shield equal to half of Keichitsu's DEF plus 100 again. When a target afflicted with "Hide the Pain" takes damage, gain 1 Punchline.
Keichitsu's provided Shield cannot exceed Keichitsu's Max HP.
## Talent

While Keichitsu holds Certified Banger: Keichitsu's ATK and DEF are increased by (Certified Banger / 200)%.
## Elation Skill
Shatter Stardust - Bounce
Hits a random enemy for 20% Ice Elation DMG and 5 Ice Toughness Damage, 8 times. Keichitsu receives 1 Certified Banger for every 30 Shield possessed by all party members.
## Ultimate
Warmth in Trying Times - Blast
Gains 6 Punchline. Deals Ice DMG equal to (Elation / 2 + 50%) of Keichitsu's ATK to the main enemy, and half as much damage to adjacent enemies.
