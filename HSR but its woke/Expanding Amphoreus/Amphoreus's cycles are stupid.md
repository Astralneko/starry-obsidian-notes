# Timelines
I'm changing the definitions for certain parts of a cycle:
- Era Luminosa: Still refers to the time before Kephale's birth.
- Era Creatura: Still refers to the time between then and the first human settlements, Okhema and Janusopolis.
- Era Chrysea: Still refers to the time up until Aidonia experiences Death.
- Era Bellica: Now that death is here, war follows soon after. Ends in Light Year 3870 with the beginning of the Flame-Chase Journey, rather than ending with Cerydra's death or exile. That being said, Cerydra and Hysilens are still from this era, as they come to power before Seliose begins the Flame-Chase.
- Era Alcyone: Refers to the period from Light Year 3870 to Light Year 4930, where strife seems to be relatively minimal unless it was borne from the Era Bellica. Canonically, this is part of the next era. However, the first half of the Flame-Chase is different enough from the second half to deserve its own designation, especially because the public's reaction to the second half of the Flame-Chase means that some would call this era separate from that one in spite of being part of the Flame-Chase.
- Era Erasa/Crepuscula: Refers to the period after Light Year 4930, where the remaining Titans die over a mere 2 years - so called because without them, the world of Amphoreus starts to forget its normal shape. Just look at 3.4 and 3.6. The prior era is called Era Halcyone in large part because the horrors that begin at this time make the general population want to go back. It does happen to be effectively the last legs of cycle-bound Amphoreus, but that's a bonus.
- Era Nova: A hypothetical time brought by the previous two eras, in Light Year 4932. Refers to a rebirth of Amphoreus - however, in genuine cycles, this era does not actually have any duration, and is immediately followed by an Era Luminosa. If it did not have zero duration, the universe would be destroyed (and no, not just the simulation). In 3.7 and 4.n, Era Nova refers respectively to the birth of Ferritumulus (Irontomb) and the Final Painting of Graphia. However, it is still seen as a good thing in all cycles, as it leads to a refresh of the world, preventing it from being destroyed in the Era Crepuscula by restarting it before destruction can finish.
