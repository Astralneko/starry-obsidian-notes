Much as March, I'd want to make Sparkle's magic plurality with her thousand masks more apparent by making it more clear that Sparxie is part of Sparkle's mind that was animated into its own body by the magic of Planarcadia. Essentially, Planarcadia made it so that Sparxie is able to have a voice, where normally Sparkle wouldn't let her have one because she isn't "the real Sparkle" (even though they're *both* the real Sparkle, as it were).

The subtitle of this page is representative of my take on the ideas presented by the tension between Sparkle and Sparxie: Sparkle constantly discards her masks, which *in canon* are part of her, because she doesn't want to be defined by them. Probably, Sparkle sees definition as shackles, preventing her from being herself. I've met real life people who are against labeling themselves for similar reasons.
Sparxie represents a defined Sparkle, as the wishes of the masses watching her not only give her power, but give her form. She's an imagenae - one formed with Sparkle's headmate as a bedrock and seemingly merged with said headmate after 4.0, yes, but still - she is the exact thing Sparkle fears. It's why I hope the remainder of 4.x doesn't gloss over the fact that these two entities probably can't just "coexist" in what now appears to be the same body, without a significant amount of reckoning between the two.

Sparxie's memetic virus is also plushie-based in this AU rather than merely an overlay on the normal body. Sparkle herself uses plushies to explode things, the Ravings music video (Sparxie's theme with lyrics) has a Sparxie plush, it's cooler than what is done in-game, and it allows for an easy way to get Aha to laugh and form [[March 7th, Living Record|Keichitsu]]. There is literally no downside I can think of to this.
