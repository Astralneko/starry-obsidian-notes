It appears that Pure Children of Anāsrava can themselves split to better record the memories they collect, based on Cyrene and the Seed of Memory on Amphoreus. Thus, I'd like to posit March 7th as a "living record" of sorts of the Astral Express's journeys. This includes the formation of multiple other Marches to better hold these memories.

Additionally, I will be referring to March 7th and her headmates under a collective name. There are six planets in HSR as of writing (The Blue/Herta Space Station, Jarilo-VI, the Xianzhou Luofu, the Penacony of Asdana, Scepter δme-13 "Amphoreus", and the Planarcadia of Benzaitengoku), and Six-Phases of ice in March. Thus, Six-Phased System will do. She is never called this in the story, as she doesn't have a system name. However, this may change on Planarcadia, as it becomes clear to March that autopilot might not be great.

The Six-Phased System currently only has 2 members: March and Evernight. However, in my AU, "HSR but it's woke", there is at least one more, Keichitsu.
# The Blue / Penacony: March 7th
This is the "original" March of sorts. It's the one the Express Crew were introduced to when the Express hit a chunk of Six-Phased Ice. Representing the [unwholesome root](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_poisons) of delusion (moha) and [wholesome root](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_factors_(Buddhism) of kindness (mettā), March 7th most closely aligns with the path of Preservation, as she wishes to preserve memories. Makes sense, considering she woke up with none.
# Jarilo-VI / Amphoreus: Evernight
Evernight is the other canon member of March's system. As she is named after the third month of the Amphorean calendar, she also goes by the native name for this month, "Oronycha". Representing the unwholesome root of attachment (rāga) and the wholesome root of wisdom (paññā), Evernight is the holder of the secrets of their mind, namely the mere fact they are a Pure Child of Anasrāva in the first place.

Evernight first manifests on Amphoreus, but this desire to protect the Express even if it kills them had to come from somewhere. I think the seed of what would become Evernight was planted when the Trailblazer Stelle was impaled on Jarilo-VI. However, I don't think March's brain fully fragmented until Constance the Dahlia rooted around in there to wipe away memories of Terminus, as Evernight's paths seem to align very closely to those of The Dahlia: Destruction and Remembrance. From there, Evernight was ready to save March from falling under the gaze of Nanook, especially after March willingly gave up her existence to Evernight.

Evernight's canon element is Ice, but I think she more closely aligns with Quantum, what with her ability to break down memories, even so far as to send them to "Oblivion". "Oblivion" I think is what Evernight calls the space in March's headspace which Evernight can access but nobody else, not even March, can access.
# The Luofu / Planarcadia: Keichitsu
This one's a non-canon member of the system, which I posit formed from the events of the Xianzhou Luofu's main story. Embers or petals, whichever it is, March caught a piece of Phantylia, in a similar vein to Fulwish with Shuhu. Now, without the heliobus herself being aware of this fact, March is probably safe from possession *from Phantylia*. However, this does make March's brain more susceptible to memetic effects, as if she were a thoughtform like Black Swan, despite not actually being one. So, you know, if [[Sparkle, Undefinable Persona|a certain shroombunny]] were to try to turn her into a plushie... this mental stress might activate Phantylia's embers once more.

Like how Nanook's gaze is what gave Evernight the power to manifest on Amphoreus, Aha would be the one gazing upon Keichitsu. She is named after the [solar term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_term) that begins on March 7th, and formed from the *hatred* (dveṣa) of Evernight, but while she is quick to anger (not unlike Evanescia when you let her go Super Saiyan), due to Abundance-alignment is at least tangentially related to generosity (dāna).

Aha was presumably watching the Supplicants' activities, or at least Evanescia, a fellow Abundance-aligned power, would be doing so by default, with Aha likely only caring about the highlights (let's just say if anything qualifies as such, then SparxiCon is a highlight). They'd have seen March's system in the process of being turned into a plushie... and March 7th enjoying it somewhat, as she likens it to the sensation of being in a jellyfish sentinel (like what Evernight does to her on Amphoreus), a comfy thought (excluding the fact Evernight forced her to do it). Meanwhile Evernight *hates* it, as she does *not* like her agency being stripped unwillingly (Six-Phased System members fronting is fine because Evernight can probably take over if needed, but this is not true of being a Sparxie plush). Aha finds the juxtaposition of March and Evernight's reactions to the Sparxie Virus hilarious as THEY gaze upon the scene, causing Keichitsu to awaken.

As a manifestation of what once was a heliobus, Keichitsu's most aligned type and path would most likely be Fire/Abundance. Elation pathstriders seem to have to be Supplicants at least on Planarcadia, but as she was gazed upon by Aha, Keichitsu would also be at least loosely tied to the Elation.
