#worldbuilding #spectradom 
Kanvas are a sapient anthropomorphic species from [[Spectradom]]. They are primarily based on cats, and so will fall under the [[Felid]] group, but kanvas can also resemble non-felids, such as foxes and jackals. Specific kanvas will, when noted, list the real world animal they most closely align with.
# Etymology
## Literary etymology
The name "kanva" is a play on the English word "canvas".
## In-universe etymology
The name "kanva" comes from the [[Liðakuin]] word of the same meaning,  *känvakr*, which is loaned from [[Classical Nenta]] *qenava*, simply meaning "person" or "people".
# Characteristics
Kanvas are anthropomorphic furred creatures that tend to be about as tall as a human. Although typically described as feline or felinoid, some kanvas have ears or tails that more closely resemble canine or vulpine ones.
Unlike [[sketch]]es, kanvas natively have [[mahosin]] embedded in their bloodstream. This is also what gives kanvas their distinctive [[kanva color|color]]: kanvas' fur is monochromatic based on the dominant mahosin flavor in their body. The black and white flavors of mahosin is only able to be dominant if all other flavors have little to no presence; this makes the corresponding melanistic and albino kanvas exceptionally rare, despite their prevalence in the pantheons of various kanva cultures' mythology.
Naturally producing mahosin means that kanvas are able to use magic without a [[Swatch|swatch]] by using their body as the medium, a practice usually referred to as "blood magic" (as the mahosin that allows for this effect also colors kanvas' blood). However, kanvas can only use one type of magic - their dominant type - unless they supplement their blood magic with a [[Swatch|swatch]] or another such artifact. Additionally, using blood magic at all draws from their own energy reserves, so kanva mages usually "bulk up" along the same vein as real-world bodybuilders, so that they have more energy to use.
The six non-neutral mahosin flavors also result in six races (or "chromas") of kanva, in direct parody of scientological mythology. These are understood as races rather than subspecies, as when kanvas of different chromas can mate, their children will likely have a chroma that is a mix of those of their parents, fully making the fur color analogous to skin tone and hair color in humans. Still, in such a case, each child will generally only have one of the two parents' blood magic, dependent on which flavor of mahosin is predominant in their body. The predominant flavor is pseudorandom (in the sense that a kanva's "flavor profile" is deterministic, but dependent on enough factors as to appear random) and can vary with age, especially if two or more flavors started out with a similar prevalence.
