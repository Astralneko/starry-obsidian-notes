#valkyrie-world #valkyrie-species 
Valkyrie is inhabited by three broad groups of species, land-dwellers, sea-dwellers, and sky-dwellers.
# Land
Land-dwellers, common name skogsrå, are those that cannot live in the water, nor traverse the sky, but can live on the water and on dry land.
## Fiends
Fiends are considered land-dwellers, but inhabit the space between realms, and also appear in [[National Mana League]]. Powerful ones can rend space, transporting themselves or objects near-instantly.
# Sea
Sea-dwellers, common name sjörå, inhabit the oceans and aquifers of Valkyrie. Notably, there is a huge aquifer, known as "Valkyrie's Belly", with a large sjörå population.
# Sky
Sky-dwellers, common name bergsrå, are similar to land-dwellers in that they require air, but unlike them, they can naturally traverse the sky. Their common name comes from their association with mountains, natural high places, but they often inhabit highlands all over Valkyrie.
