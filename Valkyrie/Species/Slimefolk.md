**Slimefolk** are a species of usually-fluid anthromorphs. While they can be made of organic materials, they are considered non-organic, like automatons and [[poltergeists]], because they need not be.
# Mechanics
Slimes are essentially the magic equivalent to mushrooms. The extremely high preponderance of [[Æther|æther]] on Valkyrie means that occasionally, enough of it collects on objects that they turn into living beings; these create both slimes (when associated with a fluid) and poltergeists (when associated with a solid).
