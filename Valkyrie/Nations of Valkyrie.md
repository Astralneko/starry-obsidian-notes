# Angelwing Alliance
The [[Angelwing Alliance]] inhabits the northern reaches of Valkyrie. Ruled by the bergsrå nation [[Sunbacked Crown]], the Sunbackers (WIP name) and their vassal states form arguably the second-most powerful nation on Valkyrie.
# Makrivalkvria Union
The [[Makrivalkvria]] Union, usually just referred to as Makrivalkvria, is a confederacy in Valkyrie's Belly. As a result, it is the economic center of Valkyrie, as most, if not all, trade has to pass through Makrivalkan territory.
