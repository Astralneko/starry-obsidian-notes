# Basic mechanics
Base Stats: HP 1088, ATK 588, DEF 488, SPD 88, Taunt 98, Energy 208
## Basic Attack
Jealous Ōnusa - Single target
Deals damage equal to 30-60/84% of Reimu's ATK. If the target has at least one stack of "Coda", applies one stack of "Coda" to the target.
## Skill
Hot Take - Blast
Deals 10% of Reimu's Max HP in self damage. The main target takes damage equal to 50-95/120% of ATK if the target has no "Coda" stacks, or 70-135/170% of ATK if they do; adjacent targets take 60% of the damage taken by the main target. The target also receives 1 stack of "Coda", plus an additional 1 stack of "Coda" for every DoT effect on the target (including things like Guinaifen's "Burnkiss" or Hysilens's Boon "Swan Song"). If the target has at least 10 stacks of "Coda" after using this attack, their Action is advanced by 1 point for every stack of "Coda" above 10 that they have, up to a maximum of 50 (half a cycle). If selecting a target that has at least one stack of "Coda", the skill button appears powered up and Reimu's ōnusa produces embers.
## Talent
Burning Recursion
"Coda" may stack up to a maximum of 64 per target. "Coda" is considered Burn DoT, but does not deal Burn DoT damage by default. This means that for example, increasing its stack count triggers Acheron's Talent. An enemy target inflicted with "Coda" receives extra Toughness damage when attacked. "Coda" is removed 300 cycle points after they are last applied, unless more stacks are applied. "Coda" is not removed if Reimu is killed or removed from play (such as via Khaslana's Ultimate).
## Ultimate
D.S. al. Coda - Blast
Deals 30 Toughness damage to the main attacked enemy regardless of if they are weak to Fire, and deals 15 Toughness damage to the adjacent attacked enemy. If the main attacked enemy has more than 32 stacks of "Coda", instead deal 20 Toughness damage to the adjacent attacked enemies, and add the enemies adjacent to the adjacent enemies as targets; new targets added in this way take 10 Toughness damage regardless of if they are weak to Fire. Deals damage based on 100-200/250% of Reimu's ATK to the main target, with other targets taking 20-40/50% less damage times the distance the target is from the main target. If the target is already Weakness Broken, deal 30%/20%/15%/10% of the original damage as additional True Damage, depending on how much Toughness damage the target was supposed to take. All DoT effects, including "Coda", are triggered after the attack. When "Coda" is triggered, trigger 1 instance of Burn Damage for every 8 stacks of "Coda" on the target, then remove all "Coda" stacks on the target.
# Ascension
## A2
Broken Record, Skipping Disc
When an enemy's Weakness is Broken, they gain 1 stack of "Coda".
## A4
Piteous Recharge
Reimu's Ultimate heals her by 10% of her Max HP.
## A6
Meaningless Cycles
When an enemy takes Toughness Damage from a Nihility unit in the party, they gain 1 stack of "Coda".
## E1
Dotted Quarter
When an enemy receives or takes damage from a DoT effect, they gain 1 stack of "Coda".
## E2
Half Sharp
If a target has at least 8 stacks of "Coda", they take Burn DoT damage on their turn.
## E3
Mezzo Forte
Skill Lv. +2, up to a maximum of Lv.15. Basic ATK Lv. +1, up to a maximum of Lv.10.
## E4
Whole Note
The maximum number of "Coda" stacks on a target increases to 128.
## E5
Fortissimo
Ultimate Lv. +2, up to a maximum of Lv.15. Talent Lv. +2, up to a maximum of Lv.15.
## E6
Sesquisharp
If a target has at least 16 stacks of "Coda", they take Burn DoT on their turn for every 16 stacks of "Coda" they have.
