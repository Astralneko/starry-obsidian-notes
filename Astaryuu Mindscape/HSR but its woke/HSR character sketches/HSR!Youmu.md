# Basic mechanics
Base Stats: HP 1137, ATK 376, DEF 594, SPD 99, Taunt 100, Energy 250 (mixture of Evernight and Hyacine stats)
## Basic Attack
If Sakurayurei is on the field and has at least 1 HP, uses "Bifröst." Else, uses "Gjallarbrú."
"Gjallarbrú": Single. Deals damage equal to 50-80/100% of Youmu's DEF. If Sakurayurei is on the field, Sakurayurei leaves the field and the attack deals 200% more damage.
"Bifröst": Blast. Deals damage equal to 80-130/175% of Youmu's DEF. Allies with "Lovesick" receive 1 stack of "Lovesick", up to a maximum of 10, and the timer on "Lovesick" is refreshed.
## Skill
If Sakurayurei is not on the field, uses "Phantom Notification." Otherwise, if Sakurayurei has at least 1 HP, uses "Lífþrasir". The Skill cannot be used if neither of the above are true.
Phantom Notification: Support. Summons Sakurayurei to the field with HP equal to Youmu's Max HP. The targeted ally receives one stack of "Lovesick", up to a maximum of 10, and the timer on "Lovesick" is refreshed.
Lífþrasir: Single. Attacks an enemy for 40-80/100% of Youmu's DEF, as well as marking them with the debuff "Lan's Despair". (As this is legally a debuff, inflicting "Lan's Despair" can charge things like Acheron's Slashed Dreams.) Sakurayurei will target an enemy with "Lan's Despair" instead of the automatically chosen target on its next turn, and then clear "Lan's Despair". An enemy inflicted with "Lan's Despair" also takes Toughness Damage from Lightning damage even if they are not weak to Lightning.
## Talent
She Loves Me Not
When Sakurayurei receives "Finality's Blessing", Youmu removes all debuffs that would prevent herself from attacking and Action Advances by 200 points.
## Memosprite Skill
Heartbreak Defibrilate - Single
Sakurayurei consumes 25% of its own HP to deal 80-160/225% the value of the lost HP as True Damage, as well as 20% Toughness Damage, when its turn comes up in the Action Order.
## Memosprite Talent
She Loves Me
If Sakurayurei is on the field and is able to move, it takes damage instead of Youmu when Youmu is targeted. If Sakurayurei reaches 0 HP by any means, it receives "Finality's Blessing" instead of leaving the field. When Sakurayurei leaves the field, all allies with "Lovesick" have their "Lovesick" stacks cleared and Action Advance by 10 points per stack of "Lovesick".
## Ultimate
Arrow of Fate - Blast
Deals damage equal to 100-150/175% of Youmu's DEF to the primary target and 20-60/100% of Youmu's DEF to adjacent targets. Consumes 25% of both Youmu's and her memosprite's Max HP and deals the same amount as True Damage to all targeted enemies. All allies with "Lovesick" gain 1 stack of "Lovesick", up to a maximum of 10, and the timer on "Lovesick" is refreshed.
## Technique
Uses Phantom Notification at the start of battle. Like DHPT's Technique, the targeted ally for "Lovesick" is the one who attacks the enemy.
# Ascension
## A2
Phantom Follower
Attacks by Sakurayurei count as Follow-Up Attacks.
## A4
Hanarus
"Lovesick" increases the target's ATK by 10% of Youmu's DEF per stack of "Lovesick."
## A6
Chronic Lovesickness
When Enhanced "Gjallarbrú" is used, "Lovesick" is reduced to 1 rather than being cleared.
## E1
Parasocial Follower
When an ally uses a Follow-Up Attack, they and Youmu both receive 1 stack of "Lovesick", up to a maximum of 10. 
## E2
Let Me In
When any other memosprite uses their Memosprite Skill: If Sakurayurei is on the field, its Action Advances by 50 cycle points. Otherwise, Youmu's Action Advances by 50 cycle points.
## E3
Ratio Success
Skill Lv. +2, up to a maximum of Lv.15. Memosprite Skill Lv. +1, up to a maximum of Lv.10. Basic ATK Lv. +1, up to a maximum of Lv.10.
## E4
Necroposting
Sakurayurei is summoned onto the field at the start of battle. If the Technique is used, its Max HP is doubled until Sakurayurei leaves the field.
## E5
Milestone Reached
Ultimate Lv. +2, up to a maximum of Lv.15. Talent Lv. +2, up to a maximum of Lv.15. Memosprite Talent Lv. +1, up to a maximum of Lv.10.
## E6
Modern Simping
The maximum stack count of "Lovesick" increases to 16.
