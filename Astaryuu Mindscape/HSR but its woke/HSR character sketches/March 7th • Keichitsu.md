# Basic mechanics
Base Stats: HP 1080, ATK 512, DEF 586, SPD 99, Taunt 100, Energy 160
## Basic Attack
Nightly Hate-Mail - Single-Target
Gains 1 Punchline. Deal Ice DMG equal to 50-100% (140% at Lv.10) of Keichitsu's ATK.
## Skill
Lightshow Recollection - Support AOE
Gains 6 Punchline. All allies other than Keichitsu receive "Snow Knight" for 3 of Keichitsu's turns (Aha Instant turns do not count). They receive Shield equal to 10-20% (24% at Lv.15) of Keichitsu's DEF plus 80-200 (280 at Lv. 15). At the beginning of Keichitsu's action (including Aha Instant), those with "Snow Knight" receive this amount of Shield again. When a target afflicted with "Snow Knight" takes damage, gain 1 Punchline.
Keichitsu's provided Shield cannot exceed Keichitsu's Max HP. If Keichitsu uses Lightshow Recollection whilst "Snow Knight" is still active, its timer is reset to 3 turns. When "Snow Knight" ends, all Shield is removed from the targets.
## Talent
Too Close to the Fake Sun
While Keichitsu holds Certified Banger: Keichitsu's ATK and DEF are increased by (Certified Banger / 200)%. If Keichitsu has 200 or more Certified Banger, she has a much higher chance of being attacked (functionally, her Taunt is doubled).
## Elation Skill
Shatter Stardust - Bounce
Hits a random enemy for 12-20% (24% at Lv. 15) Ice Elation DMG and 5 Ice Toughness Damage, 8 times. Keichitsu receives 1 Certified Banger for every 30 Shield possessed by all party members.
## Ultimate
After Chasing Flames - Blast
Gains 6 Punchline. Deals Ice DMG equal to (Elation / 2 + 50)% of Keichitsu's ATK to the main enemy, and half as much damage to adjacent enemies.
## Technique
Sparkling Recollection
When entering the battle, Keichitsu immediately uses her Skill. If Keichitsu initiates combat, enemies weak to Ice have a 20% chance of being Frozen for 1 turn.
# Ascension
## A2
The Fluffy and the Furious
When any ally receives Shield, Keichitsu receives Shield equal to half the amount granted. If this Shield came from Keichitsu's Skill "Lightshow Recollection", Keichitsu also receives "Snow Knight".
## A4
Third Wheel
For every other "March 7th" in the team, a passive effect is given to them:
- March 7th, Preservation: March 7th's ATK is increased by (Shield / Max HP * 100)%. 
- March 7th, The Hunt: When Breaking the enemy's Weakness, the Weakness Break effect of all Combat Types March 7th's attack deals is applied.
- March 7th • Evernight, Remembrance: When an ally's Shield takes damage, Evernight receives 1 "Memoria".
## A6
Six Stars, Six Phases
For every 50 Certified Banger Keichitsu possesses, True Damage equal to 6% of the original damage dealt is added to each strike of "Shatter Stardust".
## E1
No Strangers Here
For every Fire or Elation unit on the team, Keichitsu enters the battlefield with 10 Certified Banger. If the unit is both, Keichitsu enters the battlefield with 30 Certified Banger instead.
## E2
I Know The Rules Of The Game
When Aha Instant is triggered, allies with "Snow Knight" receive 20 Energy.
## E3
A Full Commitment To The Bit
Skill Lv. +2, up to a maximum of Lv.15. Basic ATK Lv. +1, up to a maximum of Lv.10. Elation Lv. +1, up to a maximum of Lv.15.
## E4
You Wouldn't Get It
When Keichitsu reaches 200 Certified Banger, she and all enemies Action Advance by 50%.
## E5
Ask Me How I'm Feeling
Ultimate Lv. +2, up to a maximum of Lv.15. Talent Lv. +2, up to a maximum of Lv.15. Elation Lv. +1, up to a maximum of Lv. 15.
## E6
I Don't Understand Me
If the team contains three "March 7th" characters: All "March 7th" characters can deal Toughness Damage even if the enemy is not weak to their respective Combat Type. Additionally, they receive another passive buff:
- March 7th, Preservation: "Girl Power" can trigger 2 more times in the turn.
- March 7th, The Hunt: When designating a new "Shifu", the previous "Shifu" receives "Knowledge Retained". March 7th's attacks receive the Path-based effect of "Master, It's Tea Time!" for allies with "Knowledge Retained" in addition to the designated "Shifu".
- March 7th • Evernight, Remembrance: When Evey uses the Memosprite Skill "Dream, Dissolving, as Dew", her Shield is included in the tally of Max HP.
- March 7th • Keichitsu, Elation: Increases Keichitsu's ATK and DEF respectively by the average of the ATK and DEF of all allies with "Snow Knight".
