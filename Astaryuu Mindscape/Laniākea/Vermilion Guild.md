#pmdlight-institution
**Vermilion Guild** (Japanese: **朽葉色のギルド**^[Kuchiba-iro no Girudo] *Vermilion Guild*) is a first-class Guild where Exploration Teams work and train. It is also where the player starts their adventure in an Exploration Team in Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Light in the Shadows. [[Holly]] the Nekyritan is the Guildmaster. The guild's main building is in [[Torch Valley]], but there are two outposts as well: one in [[Magellan City]] and one on [[Moonlight Mountain]].

# Trivia
* The exploration teams of the Vermilion Guild are all named after constellations.
	* The teams the player can control during the story of Light in the Shadows - [[Team Lyre|Lyre]], [[Team Cepheus|Cepheus]], and [[Team Draco|Draco]] - all have pole stars at some point in the earth's precession (Vega, Alpha Cephei|Alderamin, and Thuban respectively).
	* Most of the teams the player cannot control - [[Team Orion|Orion]]/[[Team Julpan|Julpan]], [[Team Hydra|Hydra]], [[Team Cetus|Cetus]], and [[Team Ophiuka|Ophiuka]] - are named after equatorial constellations, but not those that gave their names to zodiac signs.
