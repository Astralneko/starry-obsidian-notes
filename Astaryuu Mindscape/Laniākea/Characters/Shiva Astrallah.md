#pmdlight-character 
**Shiva** (Japanese: **イザナンヤ星神** *Izananya Seishin*) is an Eevee<sup>[citation needed]</sup>, seen variously as normal and [[Special trait|alterpede]], but regardless as [[starbound Pokémon|starbound]]. They have no confirmed last name, but as both [[Sahara Astrallah|Sahara]] and [[Anahit Astrallah|Anahit]] assume they are their parent, [[Holly]] writes it up as "Shiva Astrallah".

# Plot
Shiva appears initially as a spirit in the dreams of [[Sahara Astrallah|Sahara]], although they initially appear as a starbound Eevee. They say they are related to Sahara and [[Anahit Astrallah|Anahit]], which causes the former to assume the then-Eevee is her absent mother. Anahit is much more skeptical, though.

In some parts of [[Laniākea]], Terapagos is said to be the progenitor of the startouched trait, much as with Necrozma and the gembound trait. On the flipside, though, Terapagos is stated by others to be the carrier of the world above the "chaotic seas" (the endless darkness of Necrozma), and thus probably wouldn't have time to produce startouched Pokémon.

# Trivia
* Shiva is said to have a preference for Eevee because it is one of the few Pokémon able to show complete mastery of all Pokémon types.
