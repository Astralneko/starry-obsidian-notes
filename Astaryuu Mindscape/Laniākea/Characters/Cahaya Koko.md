'''Cahaya Koko''' (Japanese: '''チャハヤ''' ''Cahaya'') is an Eevee that was brought to [[Laniākea]] by [[Paradox]], who is in the exploration team [[Team Lyre]].

# Plot
Cahaya is a 14-year-old student from an alternate world to Laniakea. She was brought there by [[Paradox]] at the beginning of Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Light in the Shadows. After wandering the desert south of [[Torch Valley]] for hours, they are found by [[Sahara Ketalla|an alterpede Quakeon]]. After introducing herself as Sahara, she takes them back to her workplace, the [[Vermilion Guild]] building in Torch Valley. Cahaya meets Sahara's Togetic teammate [[Telur Fereta|Telur]] here, and decides to join their exploration team, [[Team Lyre]].

In their first [[Mystery dungeon|dungeon]] expedition, Cahaya meets [[Sabishi Copen|Sabishi]], another member of their class that was brought to the world of Laniakea and transformed into an Eevee. This causes them to meet [[Team Orion]], a more successful exploration team working for the Vermilion Guild, which includes Sabishi within it. They antagonize Cahaya and, as Team Orion's leader [[Lazam Torobelle|Lazam]] knows them well, the rest of Team Orion as well.

Over the course of the adventure, Cahaya and the rest of Team Lyre are tasked with the collection of several crystallized light chunks found around Laniakea, however, [[Team Orion]] antagonizes them along the way and takes most of them. These, Lazam reveals, are a power source for the [[Blindlight]], a massive "light-eater" slumbering within [[Alta Mountain]]. Cahaya attempts to stop Lazam from awakening the crystalline beast, but fails, causing a calamity to start that could destroy both Laniākea and Cahaya's old world.
