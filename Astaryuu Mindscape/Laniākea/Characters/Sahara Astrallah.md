**Sahara Astrallah** (Japanese: **げっしょく星神** _Gesshoku Suterami_) is an anthropomorphic Quakeon who cofounded [[Team Lyre]] along with [[Telur Fereta|Telur]] the Togetic.
# Plot
Sahara is the Pokémon who finds [[Cahaya Koko|the player]] after they are sent across worlds. However, notably, Sahara never sleeps in the dormitory at the end of the day, unlike Cahaya and the other members of Team Lyre. Initially, she chalks this up to insomnia, as "when I dream, the dreams are weird and scary." This is later explained as being due to Sahara being a [[starbound Pokémon]]; as a result, she derives energy from starlight and so does not need much, if any, sleep.

Sahara also had a twin sister, [[Anahit Astrallah|Anahit]], but she disappeared before the beginning of the story. However, neither Pokémon knows their parents or even a time before roughly the age of nine. Most in town don't question this, as it's not uncommon to not have any self-directed memories from childhood, even if that age is still suspicious even under that explanation. Sahara, though, is convinced that the lack of memory and lack of parents are connected; her personal goal in [[Vermilion Guild]] is to eventually find her parents, or evidence of their death if they have succumbed to that.

# Trivia

- Sahara's Japanese name contains multiple notable pieces of trivia.
    - The idea of having starbound Pokémon derive energy from moonlight/starlight may have been derived from Sahara's Japanese given name - while it does mean "lunar eclipse", it literally refers to eating the moon.
    - In Japanese, Sahara's last name is spelled entirely in kanji, 星神, even though the reading of 星 is none of the standard ones, instead coming from the name of the Stellar type in Japanese. Interestingly, while the same kanji are used to write [[Shiva Astrallah|Shiva]]'s Japanese name, there, the more normal reading セイシン _seishin_ is used.
    - The reading is further altered by the removal of the が, though this is explained in an optional conversation as a way of distancing herself from [[Anahit Astrallah|her now-corrupted sister]], who maintains the original pronunciation of the name.
