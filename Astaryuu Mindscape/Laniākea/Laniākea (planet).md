**Laniākea** is the name of the world upon which Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Light in the Shadows takes place.

It is named after the Laniākea Supercluster, one of the largest defined objects, a chain of 100,000 not-quite-bound galaxies (including the Milky Way itself), spanning 250 million light years, affected by similar gravitational forces - namely the Great Attractor and the Shapley Supercluster. Laniākea itself means "immense heaven" or "immeasurable heaven", and the name refers to the entire night sky itself, much of which is at least partially covered in these galaxies.
# Geography
The planet's land is mostly contained to one supercontinent, which is itself usually referred to as Laniākea. The supercontinent resembles the real life landmasses Eurasia, Africa, and Greenland. While ocean surrounds the continent, islands can still be found in this ocean. These islands are usually volcanic in nature; off the coast of the southeastern part of the continent has the most such islands, but they are dotted throughout. The largest landmass not part of Laniākea is a pair of moderately large islands near the south pole; these are likely based on Antarctica.

While pockets of Laniākea seem to have ice year-round, and these occur mostly at extreme latitude, they are not consistent; north-central Laniākea (particularly [[Hyozan]]) has such pockets, but the northeast's ice is mostly seasonal, despite being at nearly the same latitude. Since some Pokémon are able to manipulate the weather and/or climate of an area, ironically enough, Laniākea appears to be in what amounts to an anthropogenic *ice age*. 
## Landmasses
As Laniākea is a supercontinent, almost all landforms are connected by land. However, this does not mean there aren't names for different parts of the continent. These include:
- [[Tokonia]]: The eastern part of the continent, generally defined as everything east of Meditoti, though sometimes Meditoti and Aranei are considered part of it.
- [[Josuna]]: The easternmost peninsula. Often called "the Bay of Melodies", as it is known for songbirds and being the home of the music Pokémon Meloetta.
- [[Icewall Plateau]]: The cold highland north of Meditoti. Unlike Meditoti itself, it's usually considered part of Tokonia.
- [[Aranei]]: The south-central peninsula, adjacent to the continent-like Nuburia, whose edge is the southern boundary of the Meditoti mountain complex.
- [[Meditoti]]: The center of the continent, formed by several chained mountain ranges from when the continents that preceded Laniākea merged together.
- [[Nuburia]]: The southwestern landmass. While it's tough to define, approximately 30% of the landmass of Laniākea is in some way part of Nuburia.
- [[Evrinia]]: The northwestern landmass.
