**Poltergeists** are a species of non-organic lifeform - usually, a spirit - that has become associated with an object. These can be made naturally or artificially.
# Mechanics
Generally, a poltergeist has an anthromorphic "presentation." However, these are not different "species" of poltergeist, as the presentation is not the spirit's true body (this is the object they are associated with) and the spirit themselves chooses their appearance.

The process of creating a poltergeist is known as "[[Soulsmithing|soulsmithing]]." Because poltergeists have naturally better control of æther than organics do, soulsmithing has become commonplace on Valkyrie.
