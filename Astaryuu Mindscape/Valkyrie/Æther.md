#valkyrie-world 
**Æther** is the material whose manipulation creates magical effects. It is the makeup of spirits, and Valkyrie is overflowing with the stuff.
Notably, while æther can be manipulated, it is difficult for organic life to do so. Non-organic life, such as [[Poltergeists|poltergeists]] and [[Slimefolk|slimes]], have a much easier time doing so.
