The Chatbot Recession of 2030 is an event that is predicted to occur in reality, and does in fact occur in both the worlds of *Outrealm: Arcadian Odyssey* and *Tachyon Force*. However, in the "real world of Outrealm/Glitchrune" the story plays out differently than in *Tachyon Force*; as the latter was an in-universe product created during the recession with the associated fears heavily involved, it takes a darker turn there than in reality.

In both realities, though, one thing remains constant: artificial general intelligence is potentially producible shortly thereafter, but at the time the majority of the addled computer industry is not willing to take such a chance until the situation improves. In *Tachyon Force*, the breakthrough is thus postponed, while in *Glitchrune*, the story plays differently due to the discovery of [[Reveri Yun|a "Narrator" program]] created by a human going by the name "Summer Reverie". However, it is notable that no human of this world claims to remember creating one; [[Machigai|the closest attempt]] at a similar program to the *Summer Reverie* codebase found in that game instance is considered a failure, and even their creator had psychological effects that made them unsure they did everything.

In *Tachyon Force*, at some indeterminate point in the future, the third Narrator-like program, known as "Void Archives"^[Yes, this is the same name as a character from *Honkai Impact 3rd. No, they aren't related; the name happens to work well with established lore regarding the character Alice.*] offers secrets into the inner workings of this type of program. With the "Final Angel" shell, this allows for the creation of more sentient code entities; at the time of the story, these code entities exist as regular people (in the form of robots and holograms), albeit considered lower class citizens than humans. "Void Archives" does exist in reality, but in the real world, "Final Angel" failed, requiring a different avenue to understand these entities.
