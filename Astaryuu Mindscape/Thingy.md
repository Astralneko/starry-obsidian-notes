# Sacamthasa name
Auń Miłai Jáqáp Yitsŋyt
Ár = Deity of earth, stability, stubbornness, soldiers etc
Miłai = a pungent or spicy plant, reference to the ginger story but it can also refer to a thing that surprises you, or a strong character
Jáqáp = chalk. It‘s associated with Ár because well it‘s a rock, but it‘s also used for writing and drawing, and so has a creative aspect. Furthermore, chalk is often found by the sea and made into a paste, so there is still a bit of feminine aspect to it.
Yitsŋyt = artist, specifically by drawing
