Ár = Deity of earth, stability, stubbornness, soldiers etc
Miłai = a pungent or spicy plant, reference to the ginger story but it can also refer to a thing that surprises you, or a strong character
Jáqáp = chalk. It‘s associated with Ár because well it‘s a rock, but it‘s also used for writing and drawing, and so has a creative aspect. Furthermore, chalk is often found by the sea and made into a paste, so there is still a bit of feminine aspect to it.
Yitsŋyt = artist, specifically by drawing

# Rauratoshan to celebrate Sharairaza sometime soon
-# Ruviyatangan Ŋorld-Herald
Tomorrow, 15 Kastarita, marks the national observance of Sharairaza, the day of harvest in Rauratoshan. The full moon is not until a week from now, so it's expected that 21 Kastarita will be a day off for Rauratoshanians that don't work for the government.

Sharairaza also marks a deadline for the practically-locked up Parliament, whom have been deliberating over elections and Parliament's own structure for the majority of the last month. With Pekoyarit's suggestion still being deliberated, this means it is unlikely that we will enter Korongrafi with six provinces.

Ruviyatangan's preparations for Sharairaza are under way. The Zanmalaté Observatory at our outskirts will hold a free moon-watching event on the 21st, where selenography and miscellanea about our neighborhood in the stars will be discussed. Tomorrow, RARK students will have a berry-picking day, where participants can keep up to 1.5 *jega* of results per person. In Remulapola, Irikang Concert Hall will host a music event-
```
PALOMA: Javital, what are you doing up here so late, Létakkíla is still adolescent...

CARAMELLA: I could ask you the same, Paloma.

PALOMA: Whatever, tell me.

CARAMELLA: ...I've been having... Strange thoughts lately. [They adjust their top, a simple black tank top. The Quing only wears their regalia in public.]

CARAMELLA: Hah... Not quite. I... think I understand that one guy. Bogonaita, his name was?
```

# Strange incident occurs at Aristoska Stand, Owehaïá Island
-# Palontana115 Afternoon Report (English version), 10/23/2026 (2 Rokorita, Kanata 101)

Quing Regent of the Rauratoshan Kingdom, Nozomi Caramella, was seen last night with the "Daybreaker". She stabbed it into the ground, which caused a pillar of *seláku to ruvíga*¹ to form. Following this, the island was surrounded by plant life, engulfing the entire island and containing the *ruvíga*.

It is unknown what is happening on the island, inside the wall of trees and nettle; the doors out to the relevant part of the castle are currently locked. The Quing's retainer, Princess Kalia Paloma of Palontana, did accept an interview relating to their actions:

```
Video feed of an interview between Shuwu Fuluísha and Kalia Paloma. The interview is in Rokadong, but subtitled.

FULUÍSHA: Did you notice any strange actions in the Quing preceding last night?

PALOMA: Well, for the last couple weeks, she's been muttering about "choices mattering" and stuff.

FULUÍSHA: Interesting... Any spiritual concerns, madam Paloma?

PALOMA: Well, she did claim to be seeing things. Namely, moving black masses in the throne room.

FULUÍSHA: Did she describe these masses? Were they similar to the *falaisu to ruvíga*² last night, if so?

PALOMA: Yes, but no. She said they were *kawil*³, but appeared quite... drippy. They also talked in gibberish, staticky sounds.

FULUÍSHA: ...In...teresting... Well, she seems to have made a ball of nettle around the island. Care to burn it down for us?⁴

PALOMA: Absolutely the fuck not. What, do you want to risk harming or killing the Quing Regent??

FULUÍSHA: Point taken. I guess we'll have to go in the back door.
```

Paloma, however, ordered Irisela Castle's staff to keep the doorway between the castle and Aristoska Stand locked for the time being. Thus, no further investigation of Owehaïá Island can be made right now. She did announce that she would allow it soon, though.

-# ¹Abyss magic; in Pezen lore, this is space-aligned power associated with the color black or, in Debazi, *skayya* (associated with ~380nm light, and translated into Rokadong as *ruvíga*).
-# ²Tower (*falaisu*) of the above.
-# ³Pixie-like creatures from Pezen; most commonly resembling rabbitfolk, but can take many forms. Shuwu Fuluísha is one.
-# ⁴The Paloma family specializes in fire magic.
