#worldbuilding #spectradom 
The **Odafia super-eruption** is an event in the history of [[Spectradom]] on the island of the same name, [[Odafia Island]]. It occurred just over 1500 years before the events of [[Drawn to AfterLife]].

# Incident
On [[Rokaselan calendar|Towanrita 30, 929 MK]], a massive boom, likened to a thunderclap in broad daylight, was heard around the world. The main volcano of Odafia Island exploded, an incident which took five to six days to resolve. The landscape of Odafia Island was significantly altered, and much of the island was flooded with ignimbrite and/or lava, while most of Graphine and parts of northern North Gomensai were covered in 5-10cm of ash. In real-life terms, this is comparable to the Tambora eruption of 1815, albeit with strength that is likely enough to push it into VEI-8 like the [Toba supereruption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youngest_Toba_eruption). 

This volcano appears to have been in the eastern part of Odafia Island, with the explosion significantly widening Matte Bay, the stretch of water between the island and mainland Graphine.

# Effects
The eruption dumped thousands of tons of ash into the sky, mostly falling onto western [[Graphine]]. It caused a worldwide volcanic winter. Depending on the location, this lasted from only a couple years to a few decades. Rauratoshan, for example, recorded suspiciously cold winters of 929 through 932. However, in the present, the climatic effects of the eruption have been long over. While [[Quillan]] and [[Mangell]] did hear the eruption and feel the climatic effects, they were not *directly* damaged by the eruption, thanks to being separated by water.

Having effectively glassed and replaned the island, the eruption and its consequences rendered Odafia Island effectively uninhabitable for 10-20 years, with most residents of it and nearby islands moving to the mainland, sometimes violently. Between this and the resultant famines across Graphine and the world at large, Graphinian trade networks were virtually destroyed. Ultimately, this collapsed the state of [[Shadar]].

# Trivia
- The direct effects of the Odafia supereruption may have long since ended, but there is still some noticeable things that come from it. Namely, fiction keeps the eruption close to mind, with the Pezenite equivalent of medieval fantasy often involving fighting bandits, barbarians, and the like "from the west" (i.e. from the Matte Islands).
* The [[Shadaric calendar]] supposedly uses the super-eruption as its epoch. However, in that case, the time is incorrect due to missing or ignored information.
