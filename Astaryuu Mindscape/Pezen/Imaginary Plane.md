The **Imaginary Plane** is a dimension in [[Pezen]] that allows for the use of magic whilst preserving conservation of energy.

It is usually depicted as lateral to the Real Plane, but like the other definition of *imaginary*, it appears that mortals can influence the Imaginary Plane, particularly via thought, language, and other forms of expression.

Conservation of energy only applies if the system is closed. When considering both the Imaginary and Real Planes, the universe of Pezen *is* closed. However, to mortal beings, whom can only see the Real Plane, effects that transfer energy from one plane to another appear to create or destroy energy.

Energy tied to the Imaginary plane is called Imagination energy. While Real objects can't pull Imagination energy without special materials, they can still influence Imagination energy in weak, but existent ways. Most importantly, most forms of mortal expression are known to carry some weight on the Imaginary Plane, perhaps because the mental impulses are highly energetic.

"Spirits" are mechanically beings that primarily interact with the Imaginary Plane. These can be brought into the Real Plane, created from Imagination energy, or dispersed into Imagination energy using the [[Four Sacred Treasures]]. *Kawil* and *zombrunnserents* (in English: quills and shavings) are varieties of spirits that have become endemic to the Real Plane. They were brought there by their respective Sacred Treasures the Mistborn Brush and the Shield of Perfection long, long ago (probably tens of thousands of years).
