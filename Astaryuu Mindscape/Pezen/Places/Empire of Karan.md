#spectradom #worldbuilding #karantank 
The **Empire of Karan** (Rokadong:    *Surahánbi o Karlan*) is a former state that claimed the majority of [[Quillan]]. It was formed from the Nentan Kingdoms in 1626 MK (Remuymaga 406) in the [[Neneta Peninsula]], and extended across most of mainland Quillan over the coming centuries.
# History
## Nenta Kingdom
The Empire of Karan started as one of the Nentan Kingdoms, known as Karantank and founded in 1505 MK (Remuymaga 285). As Karantank started taking over its neighbors, the country was renamed to the Empire of Karan in Remuymaga 406.
## Imperial Karan
Karantank was very successful in general, taking over most of Quillan at its peak in 1879 MK (Karlan 171), including the majority of modern-day [[Quill Kingdom]] and [[Saygen]]. Around this time, they became economic partners with what would become the [[Rudafian Alliance]].
However, shortly after this peak, the Empire of Karan's economy crashed as a result of repeatedly failing to conquer the southern [[Linnekarta Mountains]], in tandem with their southern neighbors refusing to trade with them as a result. This caused many states to secede and claim sovereignty once more, such as [[Rauratoshan]] and [[Palontana]]. Ultimately, what was left of Karanian power was used to dismantle the royal rule that seemed to lead the country astray, forming the [[Republic of Karan]] in 2072 MK (Dúrisa 48).
Shortly after this fall, the powers of [[Kairi Island]] and the [[city-states of Quillan]] formally combined into one economic power, the [[Rudafian Alliance]], while Quillan itself lost substantial power on the world stage.
## Legacy
The legacy of the Empire of Karan and its collapse did remain for some time. Most of the resulting countries were not in great condition for some time following the collapse, but some were able to stabilize themselves. Most successful were the Republic of Karan themselves and the Rauratoshan Kingdom, the latter of which would try to recreate the Empire of Karan's prosperity through the [[Quill Kingdom]].
The third-to-last dynastic period of the [[Rokaselan calendar]] before the present of [[Drawn to AfterLife]] is known as "Karlan", as at this time, Rauratoshan was part of the Empire of Karan.
# Culture
Karantank both was strongly affected by and strongly affected almost the entirety of Quillan culturally, and the Empire of Karan was too vast for a simple summary to suffice. It would be easier to understand each of the states of the empire separately. However, some elements are widespread enough to serve as a form of "imperial Karanian culture".
The Empire of Karan had a heavy mark on the artistic languages of Quillan. Imperial-era sculptures are found in many cities in the [[Acrylla Archipelago]] and the [[Neneta Peninsula]], serving as both landmarks and markers of the history of the area. Karanian scholars were also responsible for codifying the [[gérimanna]] ("soundthinking") theory, which is a superset of musical traditions across the Empire of Karan that serves as a benchmark for modern music traditions across Quillan. While there are also local artists of all types from the Imperial epoch that see importance, the Empire of Karan is often given credit for their work, or at least is seen as important context to understanding the work.
