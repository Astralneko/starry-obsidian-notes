Nenevesian may be either written using the Latin alphabet or one of two conlang scripts: the native [castedan](https://arcadianodyssey.miraheze.org/wiki/Castedan "Castedan"), or the loaned [telajang](https://arcadianodyssey.miraheze.org/wiki/Telajang "Telajang"). A system adapting [razhidan](https://arcadianodyssey.miraheze.org/wiki/Razhidan?action=edit&redlink=1 "Razhidan (page does not exist)") to Nenevesian also exists, but is unpopular compared to castedan or telajang.
