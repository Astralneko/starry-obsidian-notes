**Nyuqányu** (Rokadong  [ɲùˈ(w)áɲû]) is the largest consumer electronic mail service for the [[Quill Kingdom]]. It began in 2413 MK. It is currently owned by [[Raulineshah Teska]], who bought it out in 2420 MK in a bid to wrest control from [[Debaz|Debazasri]] shareholders. They also added Nyuqányu Maria, a suite of file sharing/storing features.
# Etymology
Nyuqányu is a portmanteau of "nyuhar ta lanyur", "sending and receiving," with the word "maria" being a [[Debazi]]-loaned word meaning "data".
