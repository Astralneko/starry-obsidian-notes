Base "Meditate" - For their action, the bearer may spend Resolve to recover Vitality or Mana.

L1 "Mind over Matter" - When the Monk uses a Phys skill, they may, for 2 Energy or 2 Resolve, alter its pool to Res. You must choose between this skill and "Matter over Mind".

M1 "Follow-Through" - If being attacked with a Pummeling attack, the Monk may, as a reaction, spend 1 Energy per 3 Energy the attacker used on their turn. Both must then make an Acrobatics check; if the attacker's result is less than the Monk's result, then the Monk takes no damage. If the attacker moved in the same turn, they move an additional 10 feet in a direction chosen by the Monk.

R1 "Bread and Butter" - The Monk may spend 2 additional Energy when using a Pummeling skill. If they do so, they can use another Pummeling skill afterward, including the same skill.

L2 "Matter over Mind" - When the Monk uses a Phys skill, they may, for 2 Energy or 2 Resolve, gain Phys Resistance, but Res Weakness. You must choose between this skill and "Mind over Matter".

R2 "Rigid Stance" - Phys:Pummeling skill. For 8 Energy, the Monk does as the name implies. Until their next action, they gain Phys Resistance and only fly half as far when knocked back.

L3 "Memetic Form" - For 12 Energy, you may summon a "Sprite" if they are not out. For 8 Energy, you may depart the Sprite if they are out. The Sprite and source share pools, but while the Sprite is out, it has Phys Immunity and its source has Res Immunity; this overwrites the effect of "Matter over Mind" if present.
The source can still spend Resolve, but cannot use skills whose pool is Res. Though Phys skills can still be used by the Sprite, their pool becomes Res, and their element becomes Force if their element was Phys-aligned (Pummeling, Smashing, Impact, Piercing, Slashing, or Chopping).

Definitions:
- Partner Creature: The creature that has been tamed by the bearer of this Brand.

Base "Animal Connection" - Once per Recollection, outside of combat, you may search for a Common or Elemental Beast or Monster in the area the Tamer is currently in; if the Tamer's Will is greater than the creature's Ego, they can choose to make the beast or monster become their Partner Creature, replacing any already-existing Partner Creature. If they do not succeed or the Tamer does not make, what the beast or monster does is up to the DM.
Tamed creatures can be summoned or recalled in combat for the Tamer's action. The Tamer controls the tamed creature by default, and the tamed creature ranks up alongside the Tamer. Depending on the DM, you may be allowed to start with a tamed creature, but it is recommended not to circumvent "Speak to the Trees/Dead/Myths" with this.

L1 "Fortify Spirit" - Magi:Earth skill. For 8 Energy, you may choose to either give you or your Partner Creature a +50% buff to Fortitude until the end of the turn, or recover 3 Mana for you or your summon.

M1 "Call Spirit" - Reso:Sonic skill. For 12 Energy, you can call to their Partner Creature as long as they're within 60 feet of you, giving them a +25% buff to their Strength and Will until their 2nd action after using this skill. This will not stack if used multiple times in a row. This must be the only action used this turn, other than movement.

R1 "Drain Spirit" - Magi:Decay attack. For 6 Energy, deal damage equal to Spell Power and recover Vitality for either yourself or your Partner Creature equal to half the damage dealt, rounded up.

LL2 "Speak to the Trees" - "Animal Connection" can also be used on minor Natura and Celestia.

L2 "Call to Life" - Magi:Earth skill. For 10 Energy, the bearer pulls a wall or other such shape from the earth nearby. If a player or enemy is on the rising earth, they take Magi:Impact damage equal to twice the Magic Power of the attack. Unless the enemy is flying or has some other way to ignore terrain, this skill can prevent them from reaching the Tamer or their summon.

R2 "Call to Death" - Magi:Decay skill. For 10 Energy, the bearer draws life force from the surroundings, and causes vines to grow out of the earth in front of them. Those who touch the vines receive 4 base Phys:Decay damage, and if this occurs, the Tamer or their summon may gain 2 Vitality. The vines wither away if they have not been touched for 2 consecutive Tamer actions after being placed.

RR2 "Speak to the Dead" - "Animal Connection" can also be used on minor Undead and Spirits.

Ultimate "Speak to the Myths" - "Animal Connection" can also be used on Dragons. All Magi-pool attacks deal double damage to Mythica enemies.

Left: Will → Misdirection

Energy values are included for skills that have a combat use and are expected to take time; this is simply mechanically quantifying how much effort the skill is expected to take.

Definitions:
- **Intimidated** (Status Condition) - The afflicted's will to fight is reduced. In combat, they take 50% more Mental damage until the end of their turn. Outside of combat, they are less likely to initiate combat while Intimidated, and the effect lasts until it is deemed acceptable in roleplay.
	- Like **Aggravated**, this is intended to not be exclusive to the Performer's Brand. However, the Performer's Brand specializes in this effect (and the **Aggravated** effect) to direct the flow of combat and adjacent situations; the Brand also focuses on providing natural avenues to abuse the **Justification** mechanic, though all Brands can technically abuse this mechanic, of course.
	- **Roleplay recommendation**: Certain enemies (or players!) may decide not to attack while Intimidated, even in combat. This is, of course, not strictly necessary, nor is it expected to be universal.

L1 "Card Trick" - Perform one of a variety of random tricks, using household items or other small items on your person.
- This effect costs 6 Energy times the number of hands deemed necessary to perform the trick in combat.
- In combat or situations that could lead to combat: If your Control plus any Justification is higher than the target's Will, the target's attention is drawn toward you. They may also not see you as a threat, and thus not be aggravated toward you, although this is ultimately situation-dependent.
- Using a skill from another Brand that can help you with the trick, such as using Animator skills to build a house of cards with telekinesis, counts as Brand Justification.

M1 "Scary Feat" - Perform a trick that may appear to be dangerous. A real-life example that most adventurers can probably perform involves pretending to do something dangerous with their weapon(s), such as juggling them or the famous sword-swallowing technique.
- This effect costs 1 turn to use in combat.
- If the sum of your Will and Control is not high enough, this skill can fail, dealing hazard damage to the user based on the items used. For example, hitting oneself with their weapon may deal half the Strike Power of the weapon.
- If this skill does succeed, attention is drawn toward you. Enemies may also be **Intimidated** by this effect, causing them to take Mental damage immediately equal to the user's Word Power, and have Mental Weakness until the end of their turn.

R1 "Helping Hand" - The user shouts or otherwise calls to an ally, invigorating them to perform better feats.
- This effect cannot be used on an ally with 0 Resolve.
- The ally's next action gains positive **Opportunity Justification**.

LL2 "Look At This" - A magical light or other illusory effect draws the eyes of one or more targets who can see it; their attention is drawn toward whatever object the effect moves towards.
- This effect costs 6 Energy in combat.
- If the effect moves toward a player and the target is in combat or in a situation that could lead to combat: The target's attention is drawn toward the player; if in combat, this applies the **Aggravated** status.
- If the effect moves toward an object and the target is in a situation that could lead to combat: Their attention is drawn in that direction; the Performer and allies within a 16 foot radius of the Performer have their **Cover Level** increased to 1 if it was not already 1 or higher.

L2 "Going All In" - With reckless abandon, the user attempts their intended action, hoping for Lady Luck to smile upon them.
- The user's next action gains **Fluke Justification**.
- However, if they still fail the action, the user takes Mental hazard damage equal to 0.5x Word Power.

R2 "Sonic Boost" - Using magic, the user amplifies the power of their voice.
- This effect costs 8 Energy in combat and lasts until the end of the turn, boosting the damage of Word skills (including the Basic Word) by 50%. However, in combat, it also gains an **Upkeep** cost, where you can spend 2 Energy to continue the effect until the next turn, instead of having to reset the effect with 8 Energy. If the user's concentration is broken by way of being attacked, **Aggravated**, or **Intimidated** before the beginning of their turn, the **Upkeep** cost cannot be paid.
- The Performer's voice carries farther than normal while the user concentrates on this skill.
- If performed particularly well (e.g. the user has high Will, or uses it against a target with a low Will, especially with Justification such as using it to shout at supernatural volume), the enemy can become either **Intimidated** or **Aggravated** toward the user. Otherwise, they generally become **Aggravated** toward the user.

RR2 "Guiding Hand" - The user creates an illusory hand, which can direct allies.
- In combat, Guiding Hand costs 4 Energy and may target both an ally and an enemy. When in an attack that involves that enemy, the ally receives +5 Accuracy and +5 Dodge.
- Guiding Hand may also increase Fortitude, Will, Mystique, or Control for an ally's next check by 5, regardless of if they are in combat.
- Only one of the above effects of Guiding Hand can occur per use.

LL3 "Stunt Double" - The user creates an illusion of themselves, which can perform simple actions as if they were the real person.
- This costs 1 turn to use in combat, and cannot be used unless the user is currently hidden.
- A "Stunt Double", an illusion whose form is identical to that of the user, is created at a spot of the user's choice within 12 feet of the user. The Stunt Double is not part of the user's Batallion and has 0 Vitality, Mana, Resolve, and Energy.
- In combat or situations that could lead to combat, the Stunt Double may **Aggravate** enemies toward the Stunt Double depending on their actions.
- The Stunt Double cannot perform any attacks, but can perform any basic tasks the user can do, including Racial Passives and the like. For example, the Stunt Double can walk (or fly if the user is able to do so) toward a target, perform illusory actions (including using Card Trick or Scary Feat without paying the Energy cost, as long as the Stunt Double is not dispelled), or perform skillful, but otherwise mundane feats like dancing or attempting to pick locks. All items used in any of these actions by the Stunt Double are themselves fake, though like the Performer's other illusion magic, real, magical effects can result.
- If the user is able to move to the Stunt Double, they may choose to dispel the Stunt Double (on their turn if applicable) and move (either by running or using a movement skill) to the Stunt Double's location.
- If the user could not make it to the Stunt Double in 5 optimal turns, the Stunt Double is dispelled immediately.
- Any attack dispels the Stunt Double. For the Stunt Double specifically, close scrutiny counts as a Basic Word attack that deals 0 damage and dispels the Stunt Double, but any attack skill also has this effect.

L3 "Shining Golden Coin" - The user tosses a gold coin into the air.
- The coin lands anywhere within a 32-foot range of the user. This range may be increased by Strength or Brand skills.
- If creatures see this action, their attention is drawn toward the coin.
- The Stunt Double can perform actions with the coin, including tossing it again. The coin is immediately dropped directly downwards if the Stunt Double is dispelled while holding it.
- If the user is able to move to the coin, they can move to the coin's location at any time, assuming it is either not their turn, or their turn before any movement. This can be performed with any applicable movement skills. However, this immediately **Aggravates** all creatures whose attention was on the coin toward the user.

M3 "Phantom Thief's Gambit" - Using illusion magic, the user disguises their own body.
- This skill cannot be used in combat unless all of the following happen: 1) The user cannot be seen when they declare this skill. 2) The user spends their entire turn on this skill.
- If used in combat, and the user is seen before the beginning of their next turn, the target that saw them receives the effect for realizing the user is disguised, as the spell is considered still in progress.
- The user must have some idea of what the disguise should look like to use this skill. If they also have some idea what the disguise should sound like, they can change their voice accordingly. Images and depictions of the disguise may not be enough, especially if the user wants to mimick their voice. Yes, *in theory*, a Performer could disguise themselves as Fran the Calling Card THEMSELF if they can justify it, though...
- The user's actual Race, Equipment, Skills, and Stats, including attributes determined by these such as Racial Weaknesses, Resistances, and Passives, do not change. This could make a successful disguise more difficult.
- If the user successfully passes off their disguise, their action against the target gains **Opportunity Justification**.
- If a target realizes the user is disguised, the *target* gains **Opportunity Justification** on all actions that involve the user until the user's disguise is dispelled. The target's next action after the user's disguise is dispelled also receives **Opportunity Justification**.
- Any attack dispels the Phantom Thief's Gambit. For the Phantom Thief's Gambit specifically, close scrutiny counts as a Basic Word attack that deals 0 damage and dispels the Phantom Thief's Gambit, but any attack skill also has this effect.

R3 "Pass the Mike" - The user fires a magical wisp at an ally, giving them the boons they have collected.
- All lingering effects of the Performer's Brand, including enemies being **Aggravated** toward them, are removed from the Performer and given to one ally within 32 feet of them. Their remaining duration is largely unchanged, and is only extended until the end of that ally's turn if they would end beforehand.

RR3 "Curtain Call" - Using illusion magic on the environment, the user tries to magically hide something (or someone) from sight.
- This skill costs 1 turn in combat.
- The illusion can only affect a volume whose length, width, and height are no more than 8 feet.
- The item or ally targeted by this skill receives a **Cover Level** of 2. If the **Cover Level** already was 2, and the illusion is inconspicuous enough, then it increases to 3 instead.
- Close scrutiny causes others to be able to tell that the illusion is such. Attempting to move through the illusion, including via an attack, dispels it.
- Allies hidden by "Curtain Call" may use skills that do not demand movement outside of the illusion. When the illusion is dispelled, the ally hidden by it can, as a Reaction, use any Skill that requires no more than 12 Energy, or an equivalently fast mundane or justifiable action.
