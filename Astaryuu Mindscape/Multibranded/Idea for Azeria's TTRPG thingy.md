Base "Meditate" - For their action, the bearer may spend Resolve to recover Vitality or Mana.

L1 "Mind over Matter" - When the Monk uses a Phys skill, they may, for 2 Energy or 2 Resolve, alter its pool to Res. You must choose between this skill and "Matter over Mind".

M1 "Follow-Through" - If being attacked with a Pummeling attack, the Monk may, as a reaction, spend 1 Energy per 3 Energy the attacker used on their turn. Both must then make an Acrobatics check; if the attacker's result is less than the Monk's result, then the Monk takes no damage. If the attacker moved in the same turn, they move an additional 10 feet in a direction chosen by the Monk.

R1 "Bread and Butter" - The Monk may spend 2 additional Energy when using a Pummeling skill. If they do so, they can use another Pummeling skill afterward, including the same skill.

L2 "Matter over Mind" - When the Monk uses a Phys skill, they may, for 2 Energy or 2 Resolve, gain Phys Resistance, but Res Weakness. You must choose between this skill and "Mind over Matter".

R2 "Rigid Stance" - Phys:Pummeling skill. For 8 Energy, the Monk does as the name implies. Until their next action, they gain Phys Resistance and only fly half as far when knocked back.

L3 "Memetic Form" - For 12 Energy, you may summon a "Sprite" if they are not out. For 8 Energy, you may depart the Sprite if they are out. The Sprite and source share pools, but while the Sprite is out, it has Phys Immunity and its source has Res Immunity; this overwrites the effect of "Matter over Mind" if present.
The source can still spend Resolve, but cannot use skills whose pool is Res. Though Phys skills can still be used by the Sprite, their pool becomes Res, and their element becomes Force if their element was Phys-aligned (Pummeling, Smashing, Impact, Piercing, Slashing, or Chopping).

Definitions:
- Partner Creature: The creature that has been tamed by the bearer of this Brand.

Base "Animal Connection" - Once per Recollection, outside of combat, you may search for a Common or Elemental Beast or Monster in the area the Tamer is currently in; if the Tamer's Will is greater than the creature's Ego, they can choose to make the beast or monster become their Partner Creature, replacing any already-existing Partner Creature. If they do not succeed or the Tamer does not make, what the beast or monster does is up to the DM.
Tamed creatures can be summoned or recalled in combat for the Tamer's action. The Tamer controls the tamed creature by default, and the tamed creature ranks up alongside the Tamer. Depending on the DM, you may be allowed to start with a tamed creature, but it is recommended not to circumvent "Speak to the Trees/Dead/Myths" with this.

L1 "Fortify Spirit" - Magi:Earth skill. For 8 Energy, you may choose to either give you or your Partner Creature a +50% buff to Fortitude until the end of the turn, or recover 3 Mana for you or your summon.

M1 "Call Spirit" - Reso:Sonic skill. For 12 Energy, you can call to their Partner Creature as long as they're within 60 feet of you, giving them a +25% buff to their Strength and Will until their 2nd action after using this skill. This will not stack if used multiple times in a row. This must be the only action used this turn, other than movement.

R1 "Drain Spirit" - Magi:Decay attack. For 6 Energy, deal damage equal to Spell Power and recover Vitality for either yourself or your Partner Creature equal to half the damage dealt, rounded up.

LL2 "Speak to the Trees" - "Animal Connection" can also be used on minor Natura and Celestia.

L2 "Call to Life" - Magi:Earth skill. For 10 Energy, the bearer pulls a wall or other such shape from the earth nearby. If a player or enemy is on the rising earth, they take Magi:Impact damage equal to twice the Magic Power of the attack. Unless the enemy is flying or has some other way to ignore terrain, this skill can prevent them from reaching the Tamer or their summon.

R2 "Call to Death" - Magi:Decay skill. For 10 Energy, the bearer draws life force from the surroundings, and causes vines to grow out of the earth in front of them. Those who touch the vines receive 4 base Phys:Decay damage, and if this occurs, the Tamer or their summon may gain 2 Vitality. The vines wither away if they have not been touched for 2 consecutive Tamer actions after being placed.

RR2 "Speak to the Dead" - "Animal Connection" can also be used on minor Undead and Spirits.

Ultimate "Speak to the Myths" - "Animal Connection" can also be used on Dragons. All Magi-pool attacks deal double damage to Mythica enemies.
