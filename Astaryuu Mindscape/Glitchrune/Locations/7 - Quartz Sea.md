Chapter 7 takes place at the root of the world of *Deltaverse*, the only place in modern times where light and dark intertwine wildly without personal input. Thus, the dark-side locations (Albite Mountain, Onyx City, and Ruby Delta, which do have light equivalents but are mostly untethered to them, as well as probable dark variants of Amethyst Fields, Sapphire Bay, Jade Frontier, and Citrine Desert) are commonly referred to as the "Quartz Sea Dark World," as creating any disconnected dark worlds to this one would be dangerous to the stability of the world itself.
