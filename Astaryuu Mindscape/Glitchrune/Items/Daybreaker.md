The **Daybreaker** is one of many tools created by what might be the previous player character, [[Nacht Nullam]]. It specifically is described as Nacht's magnum opus, a large sword capable of manipulating darkness. In the game layer of "Deltaverse", its owner is also the "player character", capable of making choices.

[[Reveri Yun|The Narrator]] herself has a "Parallel Daybreaker", meaning that multiple characters have "the ability to make choices." However, through copious [[Solin Leihinheim|game breaking nonsense]], other characters (mostly Solin) can affect the world on a fundamental level if provided this ability by a Daybreaker.
# Design
The Daybreaker has a very specific, thematically relevant design. Its color palette resembles that of the maingame Chapter 2 friend Tasque Manager, offering a subtle hint to the order enforcement this sword was meant for, in case the fact *the guard is comprised of angel wings* was not enough. Interestingly, though, only one side of the blade is sharp, with the other side's blade being covered by an extension of the guard.

The blade is called the "Legendary Sword of Change" (the mathematical symbol for change being Δ, delta). Its design, with one side being blunt and incapable of damage, is a signal of its function: it allows the bearer to make meaningful choices. Of course, [[The Script|choice is abhorred in this world]], so that goes as well as expected for the bearer. Any characters that recognize the Daybreaker in [[Medina Gutierrez]]'s inventory treat her as an extension of The Script, with some preferring a dead Medina because dead dogs can't fulfill their role.
