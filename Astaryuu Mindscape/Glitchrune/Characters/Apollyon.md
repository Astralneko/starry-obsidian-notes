**Apollyon** is a [[Ruby Delta]] hellhound who at one point studied at Onyx Coleg, though unlike, for example, [[Takara]], Apollyon is not a scientist.
# Plot
Apollyon does seem to be somewhat aware that the world they are in is a game, and thus knows that [[The Script]] is factually going to happen, unless unintended actions happen. She is thus a piece in [[Yoshimitsu Jinkyuu|Dr. Jinkyuu]]'s plan to do exactly that in order to protect the world from harm.
## Prime Timeline
Before the story of Deltaverse truly begins, Dr. Jinkyuu and Apollyon agree that the latter will raise the former's amalgamate experiment [[Solin Leihinheim|Solin]].

She raises Solin to know the hero of the Prophecy, who turns out to be [[Medina Gutierrez|Medina]]. However, unlike others, she thinks that Medina may be able to be the unintended actor needed to stop the end of the world, a thought process she passes onto Solin. Thus, at first, Solin is curious about Medina and the others, helping them recover from entering the Quartz Sea Dark World.

However, Medina and Takara creating a Dark Fountain at [[Sapphire Bay]] convinces both that Medina is, in fact, bad news. After all, this is liable to upset the balance between light and darkness. This is also notable because in Chapter 1, Medina stopped the Black Beast Seiðr from doing the same, so to Apollyon, this is a betrayal.
## Glitched Timeline
While she asks who will care for the amalgamate in the glitched timeline (regarding [[LACE NULLAM|LACE]]), Dr. Jinkyuu no longer trusts Apollyon, justifying it as "a feeling that she's inadequate." Instead, he asks [[Reveri Yun|Yun]], who happily accepts; this angers the hellhound.

Apollyon ends up working against Dr. Jinkyuu in this timeline, seemingly aware from her experience in the prime timeline that if the world gets flooded with darkness, [[Reveri Yun|the Angel]] will be forced to intervene, and thus may free the inhabitants of the world from the shackles that are [[Deltaverse]]'s [[The Script|script]]. Thus, she uses her scythe to sever Onyx City from the rest of the Quartz Sea Dark World, creating the Onyx City Dark World; this does incalculable damage to both sides but does not stop the doctor and Yun.
