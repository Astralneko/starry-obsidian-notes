#glitchrune-character
**Medina Gutierrez** is the main character of Glitchrune.
# Plot
Medina is a video game streamer by the username "MesoDinosaur" on a fictional streaming service called "LiveHive". She attempts to play the game "Deltaverse", the setting of Glitchrune, but gets sucked into the game, transposed over the true main character "Medley".

The [[Daybreaker]], [[Nacht Nullam]]'s magnum opus, was already in her inventory when she arrived in the world of Glitchrune / Deltaverse. As the Daybreaker cannot be removed, this causes all sorts of problems.
