#glitchrune-character
**LACE** is the game entity hastily created in an attempt to return the glitched timeline [[Solin Leihinheim|Solin]] and [[Medina Gutierrez|Medina]] created to normalcy. They are a variant of Solin, representing the body whose soul Solin was torn from, as due to the glitches in the timeline, Nacht's soul could not actually be recovered.

Like Solin, LACE is an amalgamate. However, unlike Solin, rather than [[Apollyon]] taking custody of the chimera child [[Yoshimitsu Jinkyuu|Dr. Jinkyuu]] created, [[Reveri Yun]] does so. Additionally, without the actual human soul to bind to, LACE is not as stable as Solin.
