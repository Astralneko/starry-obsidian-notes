#glitchrune-character 
**Solin Leihinheim** (English: \[ˈsɤɫɪn ˈlæjɪnˌhæjm]) is a major character in *[[Glitchrune]]*. They are the modern incarnation of [[Nacht Nullam]].
# Plot
Solin is an "amalgamate", a being comprised of multiple souls. Their "composite soul" was created from the souls of those that fell in the [[Great War of the Worlds]], especially [[Nacht Nullam|Nacht]], who died on the trident of [[GR!Asgore|the Dark World's general]] at the same time as killing them. However, they did not have a body at first; they obtained it through experimentation conducted by [[Yoshimitsu Jinkyuu|Dr. Jinkyuu]]. Their body resembles that of Nacht as a child, as their soul was the "glue" keeping them from melting into a blur like most amalgamates.

As a "mirror" of the people around them, Solin finds it difficult to "decide things for themself". Ironic, but thematically appropriate, as in death, Nacht lost the Daybreaker, demoting them from player character. However, with aid from either the new player [[Medina Gutierrez|Medina]] or the Parallel Daybreaker-wielding [[Reveri Yun|Narrator]], they can completely [[Deltaverse crash|rewrite]] the world, making their role similar to that of actual Deltarune's Noelle. In such a position, they call themselves "Prayer 2", a deliberate reference to mistranslation that also is a pun, as Solin is well known for praying to "the Angel".

In the prime timeline, they are under the care and tutelage of [[Apollyon]], a Ruby Delta resident tasked by [[Yoshimitsu Jinkyuu|Dr. Jinkyuu]] to do so, as he trusts she is on the same page regarding [[The Script|the Script]]. In the glitched timeline, a version of prime Solin still exists, but is referred to as "[[LACE NULLAM]]", with the prime Solin appearing in place of [[GR!Ralsei]].
# Trivia
* Solin presents as female, but uses exclusively plural pronouns; this is likely due to their status as an amalgamate, as all amalgamates are referred to in this way. In English, this results in using the otherwise-genderless *they*. However, this isn't true for all languages, such as when referred to using *ellas* in Spanish.
## Name origin
Solin's first name comes from *soul* and possibly *Kaiserin*. The last name Leihinheim is obtained by reversing the Latin word *nihil* (nothing) and adding the Norse word *heim* (land).
