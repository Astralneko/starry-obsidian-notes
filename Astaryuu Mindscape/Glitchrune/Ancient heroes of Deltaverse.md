#glitchrune-groups
The '''ancient heroes of Deltaverse''' are a group of five adventurers who toured the world of [[Glitchrune]] in the distant past, during the time of the [[Great War of the Worlds]]. They were instrumental in the eventual end of that war, though one of them, [[Nacht Nullam]], died in the process.
