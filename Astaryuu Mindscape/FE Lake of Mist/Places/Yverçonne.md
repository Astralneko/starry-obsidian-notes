#fe-lake-of-mist #yverçonne
The **Kingdom of Yverçonne** is the most northerly area accessible in Lake of Mist. 
## Origin
Yverçonne is based on France. It is named primarily after Yvelines and Essonne, two of the departments of the Île-de-France region, the seat of power in the country. Yvelines contains Versailles, and thus the palace thereof, that used to be the seat of power in France.
## Geography
Yverçonne is northeast of [[Argonia]]. While it controls a small amount of Mercanti Sea coast, most of its territory is inland. Vençaille is the only city near the coast; others, including the capital Yverin, are found on river-sides. To the south and east are mountains, of which Argonia's mountain range is an offshoot, or perhaps it is the true continuation. Depends who you ask. The tallest Yverçonian mountain is Mt. Lakagígar, part of the state's eastern border.
## Relations
[[Argonia]] does have a treaty with Yverçonne that effectively make them allies, but they do NOT like each other - both just have similar goals for the time being. In fact, after Argonese prince [[Mars]] "defiles" Mt. Lakagígar by bringing the fully powered [[Fire Emblem]] back to the mountain, Yverçole declares war on Argonia.
