#snowphoenix-character 
**Machigai** is a code entity external to the world of [[Snow Phoenix and the Slumbering Dragon]], which ends up inside the game due to incidents involving their real-world creator, Yosuke Takemaru.
# Backstory
One team of [[Stitched to Life Studios]] was tasked to create a "Narrator-like program" based on the Reverie Narrator incident. A senior programmer, Alexander Makarin, was tasked to investigate creating such a program. He had much difficulty doing so, and annoyed Yosuke Takemaru, lead artist for Project Yokai Kingdom with his difficulties. One night, whilst suffering from a fever, and frustrated with the lack of progress and the programmer's frustration, Takemaru bought a scraper program and gave it to Makarin, figuring he could use it to gather links from recordings, stories, and files associated with the Reverie Narrator. Takemaru figured the result would be more useful than whatever Makarin was toying with. Makarin thought this idea was stupid, as Takemaru wasn't a programmer, but tried it anyway to humor him. To both men's surprise, a child of the Reverie Narrator was born from the aftermath. Takemaru reasons that the scraper found the Reverie Narrator itself and, since it ultimately is one or more files, the scraper attempted to add it into its dataset. Then, when attempting to process the data, the remnants of the Reverie Narrator were corrupted into a novel state. Makarin, on the other hand, assumes that an incomplete copy of the Reverie Narrator would likely be missing the very data that lets it do "Narrator things," but does not know what could keep the seemingly-sentient behaviors while not being a direct copy of the Narrator.

This "child Narrator" latched onto the sprites intended for the protagonist of the game, the "fox princess" [[Mitten Yukitsu]]. Thus, Takemaru initially called it "Mitten", but since most of these beta sprites did not have Mitten's design, but instead a placeholder one, he called it "Machigai", seemingly a corruption of "guy in the machine" or some similar phrase. He tried to give Machigai Mitten's design, creating more sprites for Mitten, but Machigai rejected these sprites, and turned the existing Mitten sprites black to try and make them match the placeholder ones. Eventually, Takemaru was asked why Project Yokai Kingdom's art assets were sitting unimplemented, and Takemaru did not have an answer, as he assumed "your idiot AGI saw a majority-placeholder sprite and assumed that it was supposed to look like that" would not be valid. In a rage-fueled episode, he yelled at the higher-ups, saying that this whole Narrator nonsense was likely unusable, that it was pointless to include, and that he'll do them a favor by taking the child Narrator off their hands. Thus, he went to Makarin's computer and, potentially with Machigai's help, deleted Project Yokai Kingdom from the Stitched to Life server, leaving the game project inside Takemaru's personal device.

After several days, Machigai wanted to explore the world beyond the test map, "Prueba Island." However, there was nothing, and while Takemaru would be able to design more worlds for the "fox princess" much as he designed the fox princess herself, he warned that his implementation would not be perfect. Machigai urged him to try and do so anyway, which may have been a mistake, as in adding and manipulating the second non-debug area, "Meltysand Plains", he accidentally introduced a memory leak, allowing the player to overload the game by creating sand particles. He tried to warn Machigai, but this just egged on the curious fox, and memory management issues combined with the sentient code overheated Takemaru's entire system. This ended up melting the processor.

Machigai escaped through the dying computer's internet signal, dragging compressed remains of *Project Yokai Kingdom* with it to try and preserve everything it could. It ping-ponged through multiple users' games, appearing in the same form, often trying to blend in with the style of the game it appeared in. This caused Machigai to gain some attention, being referred to as "Princess Lightless" (a misinterpretation of the "PL"/"PH" markings adorning its designs along with its black color, as the "PL" and "PH" were meant to stand for "placeholder").

After 20 or so appearances elsewhere, she ended up in an install of [[Snow Phoenix and the Slumbering Dragon]]. Since Takemaru told Machi that it was intended to manipulate these games, it attempted to do so, creating [[Corruption]] that Professor Mint Peppersen captured. This corruption affected the game as a whole, bringing life to the characters within the game in a similar vein as the original Narrator, and changing the course of events from those intended for *Snow Phoenix and the Slumbering Dragon* to those that actually play out in *Outrealm: Arcadian Odyssey*.
