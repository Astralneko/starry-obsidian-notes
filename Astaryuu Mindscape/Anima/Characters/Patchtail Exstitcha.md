#snowphoenix-character 
Patchtail is a character in the world of *Snow Phoenix*. In-universe, she was introduced in a side series of romance visual novels known as *Stitched Hearts*, seemingly created as a passion project of the chief developer of the original *Snow Phoenix*. She then appears in [[Snow Phoenix and the Slumbering Dragon]], as a new sidekick replacing the previous game's more slapped-together "second agent" Grant Gale.
![Patchtail ref](https://astaryuu.cc/assets/oc-art/patchtail/astaryuu-20231024.png)

# Characteristics
Patchtail is a [[bladenklaw]], a necromantically revived entity created from the corpses of dead Alarennian cat warriors, reviving their skeletons but infusing them with entirely new spirits. However, she was created at the tail end of the war between Alarenne and Neverey, and thus did not see much, if any combat; she actually settled into a Nevereyan family once peace was settled between the two nations.

This does not mean Patchtail was not created for combat, though, with these circumstances having particular effects on her biology. As she was created late in the war, she was created from a combination of multiple incomplete corpses, rather than one complete skeleton, as such complete skeletons were not super easy to find anymore. Such fusions are not as likely to succeed, but in the case of Patchtail, it worked - but it resulted in Patchtail having a set of blades from every single dead cat involved - a set of claws on the hands, a pair of serrated blades in the arms, long and sharp blades also in the arms, spikes on the feet, and fragments of a sword down her spine.

# Backstory
Once she was old enough, Patchtail's parents sent her off to the Neverey City Academy, one of the most prestigious schools in the entire world. There, she studied alchemy - Nevereyan magic - under one Professor Mint Peppersen. This caused her to meet Snow Phoenix while she was working on her proof (equivalent to a thesis, which is required to graduate from the Academy in her field), as Professor Mint had disappeared after police were notified of criminal substances being found in his possession by a colleague, and Snow was investigating this. Patchtail was a lead, because she worked with the strangely muttering Professor, but this was the first she had heard of it.

Being just as curious as Snow was, Patchtail decided to accompany Snow on his investigation. The two of them examined the notes of his that he left at his former home, and Patchtail determined they had something to do with awakening the Slumbering Dragon - a dragon that was said to have created the very land of Neverey and Al-Maho millenia past, and whose scales formed the mountain range partially dividing the two. This was strange to the pair, because that was just a myth to them. Stranger still, when a strange mist was found in the back closet, which morphed and writhed into several strange monsters, including one Machigai.

Were these monsters related to the so-called Slumbering Dragon in some way?
