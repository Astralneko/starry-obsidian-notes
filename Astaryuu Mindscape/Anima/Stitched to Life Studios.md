**Stitched to Life Studios** is the creator of the *Snow Phoenix* series, as well as a few other games. It began in the year 2032, directly following the [[Chatbot Recession of 2030]].

They are considered a "double-A studio", in the sense that they are a large company and not merely a guy or two and their commissioners and/or friends, but they are also not one of the big studios (usually considered Nintendo, Sony, Microsoft, Sega, Bandai Namco, and Atari), usually considered higher tier due to producing prominent physical media (game consoles and arcade machines) in addition to games. While their main claim to fame is the *Snow Phoenix* series, they also produce other games.
- 2032: *Snow Phoenix and the Titan's Watch*
- 2033: *Snow Phoenix and the Flameless Ghost*
- 2033: *Stitched Hearts*
- 2034: *Press H For Hero*
- 2035: *Stitched Hearts 2: Cool Summer*
- 2036: *Snow Phoenix and the Slumbering Dragon*
- 2038: *Firework Mash: Idle Holidays*
- 2039: *Fox Princess and Yokai Kingdom*
# "Narrator-like" incident
Snow Phoenix's third game was originally set for a 2035 release. However, higher-ups had heard of the "Reverie Narrator" incident, and wondered if, since they were a game studio, and a much larger one than "Summer Reverie" and unknown friends, they could create their own "Narrator". Thus, in 2034, began the project "Project Yokai Kingdom", originally set on Alaure, Anima's moon, so as to indicate it's a different world while still justifying references to Stitched to Life's other projects. This project was a beta name, as no true name was decided. Since prior experience showed that artificial intelligence seems to only work well when given existing material and told to make more of it, they planned to incorporate the "Narrator" into a game similar to *Snow Phoenix*.

The project was quickly derailed when the lead artist, Yosuke Takemaru, left the studio entirely in a manic episode. They seemed to think they "knew" their creation, and shouted at the heads of the company for not giving him the freedom to work on the "Narrator-like" as he saw fit, while also saying that the program would be completely unsuitable for use in a game project. On their way out, Takemaru deleted some of the assets from Stitched to Life's servers. The company considered suing Takemaru for theft of IP, but since "Project Yokai Kingdom" never had a proper name decided, and no game was published for outside viewing, their lawyers told them it would be an uphill battle. They ended up refocusing on the shelved *Snow Phoenix 3*, now planned to release in 2036. While this did impact development of the third game, a mostly complete, if buggy, product was released at the end of the day. Eventually, after (but "probably not related to") Takemaru's own PC was destroyed by a combination of Machigai and coding mistakes, assets from the project were discovered on the physical drives of some workstations in Stitched to Life's offices; they used these to produce "Fox Princess and Yokai Kingdom", a role-playing game in its own series, and with no "Narrator" as originally planned, but still with references to the "Snow Phoenix" series.

As it turns out, the team did, in fact, create an artificial code entity. Eventually, this entity came to be known as [[Machigai]], and shortly thereafter, it overloaded Takemaru's device It ended up in an install of [[Snow Phoenix and the Slumbering Dragon|Snow Phoenix 3]], where like the prior "Reverie Narrator", it fucked things up, seemingly giving sentience to the entities within the game while also messing with the fabric of reality (according to the view of the entity). Stitched to Life, despite initiating the project, claimed no relation to the incident, as they would not, intentionally or otherwise, release such a broken mess to the world. It would have been caught in QA, according to them.

After the incident, the team managed to recover art assets related to the project, left on physical drives instead of cloud servers, and eventually created the role-playing game *Fox Princess and Yokai Kingdom* in tandem with other developers, which at the time of the beginning of *Outrealm: Arcadian Odyssey* is the studio's latest game.
