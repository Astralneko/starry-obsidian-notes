#sfs-character 
Fallen Stars's version of Sonic arrives slightly differently; unlike the real timeline, in this timeline, [[Starfall!Zero|Infinite]] *kills* Sonic in Sunset Heights, but is redeemed by two acts:
- Acknowledging the pain he caused under the name Infinite;
- Reviving Sonic the Hedgehog.
This version of Sonic is slightly different from the one that came before, as if approximated by a new actor (or indeed, a new artist). In fact, he comes from a different alternate world where Robotnik was defeated, but his children take up their father's mantle; such would later be perpetrated by [[Starfall!Sage|Sage]].
