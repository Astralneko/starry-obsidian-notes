Much like how Japanese technically uses four scripts at the same time, Lunarian technically uses six scripts at the same time. However, most dialects do not use all six scripts at the same time. The following scripts are ordered in order of how often they are used in Lunarian writing.

1. Lunarian runes (*ƿtadassja*): An alphabet based on [man'yogana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/man%27yogana "w:man'yogana") that functions similarly to [hangul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/hangul "w:hangul"). _Ƿtadassja_ is considered the "native script" of Lunarian, and is usually written in the same direction as Sinitic or Japanese writing.
2. [Katakana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katakana "w:Katakana") (*terasja*): Generally used for loanwords from Japanese and onomatopoeia. Ironically, the name _ƿtadassja_ is often written in katakana, as it is literally "the correct characters" (with the word for _correct_ coming from Japanese _tadashii_.
3. [Hiragana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiragana "w:Hiragana") (*ønnasja*): Not used commonly in Lunatic dialects, but in Eienteyan and Impure dialects, hiragana are often used in place of katakana when the word would use hiragana in Japanese.
4. [Latin script](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_script "w:Latin script") (*romasja*): Used for non-Japanese loanwords originally written in Latin script. Use of Latin script is banned by the Lunatic Linguistic Correction Facility and a third-order misdemeanor that can result in a sentence of up to 3 years or ¥300,0000 (the highest sentence that the Lunatic Constitution allows a non-felony to have).
5. [Cyrillic script](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrillic_script "w:Cyrillic script") (*bɏlgasja*): Equivalent to Latin script, including its legal status in the Lunatic Kingdom.
6. [Hanzi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanzi "w:Hanzi") (*hansja*): Although Han characters are legal, they are considered old-fashioned in Lunatic dialects. The only common use for hanzi in modern Lunarian writing is in the numeral system.

Because the scripts see use in some dialects, there is also a complete romanization and cyrillization of Lunarian. However, only Terrestrial Lunarian uses these more than _ƿtadassja_. Both are also considered to be badly designed, with heavy use of characters outside [Windows-1251](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows-1251 "w:Windows-1251")(Cyrillic script) and [-1252](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows-1252 "w:Windows-1252") (Latin script). While there generally are commonly accepted digraphs that are within these standards, Impure Lunarian prefers to use the monographs, and so this extends to Terrestrial Lunarian.

# Lunarian runes
Lunarian runes are stacked into syllabic groups, not unlike hangul. However, it is not as regular. In general, though, most runes are read in order of where they start when moving left to right, with ties settled top to bottom. Nulls are filled with ellipses .
