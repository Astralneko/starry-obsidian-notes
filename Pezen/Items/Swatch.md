#worldbuilding #spectradom 
A '''swatch''' is a type of magic-enabling item in [[Spectradom]]. They are likened to spell cards from series like *Touhou Project*.
# Construction
The important item that makes a swatch a swatch is the use of "magic ink", which is paint imbued with [[mahosin]]. Technically, this can be applied on anything, like how technically, one can write on any surface with the right tools. In fact, historically speaking, swatches are made with the same material as whatever surface is commonly written on by the community in question - for example, palm leaves by early [[Rokasela]]ns. However, in the modern era, swatches are usually made out of heavy paper, akin to real-world card stock or paperboard.
Swatches are generally finite use, because activating the spell imbued in a swatch stresses the material the spell was printed on. However, the more durable the material is, the less likely any particular use of the spell is to break the swatch, and therefore the longer it lasts. Still, there are ancient swatches, made on clay tablets, which have survived centuries, although at a certain point, pigment has to be reapplied or the spell will become less efficient. Modern swatches are expected not to last anywhere near that length of time, but paper is easier to mass-produce, so it is what is preferred.
# Trivia
* The artist [[Sjáhn Ättesten]], a favorite of [[Flana Rénnjokuha|Flan]], notoriously painted a large mural with the same magic ink typically used on swatches, technically turning the entire wall into a swatch. However, the encoded spell only makes parts of the wall glow for several hours - useful as a streetlight, but not much else.
