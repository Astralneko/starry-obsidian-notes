*Nanni sends this exact message:*

**When you came, you said to me as follows: "I will give Gimil-Sin (when he comes) fine quality copper ingots."** 
`when 2sg.FOR come_here-REMPST 2sg.FOR say-PST this ACC 1sg.FOR 1sg.CAS give-FUT Gimil-Sin ingot~ good~ GEN copper`

*When you came here, you said this to me: "I will give Gimil-Sin great copper ingots.*

**You left then but you did not do what you promised me.**
`2sg.FOR leave-REMPST LOC this but next 2sg.FOR NEG-do-REMPST which 2sg.FOR promise-PST 1sg.FOR`
Talah yuzurkapan gun ila, ket kiha talah takkongkapan zah talah gibáran nata.

*You left at this, but next you didn't do that which you promised me.*

**You put ingots which were not good before my messenger (Sit-Sin) and said: "If you want to take them, take them; if you do not want to take them, go away!"**
`2sg.FOR place-REMPST ingot~ NEG good LOC eye GEN messenger Sit-Sin and say-REMPST if 2sg.CAS yes want take-HYP this take-COND this if 2sg.CAS NEG want take-HYP this leave-IMP 1sg.IMPS`

*You placed bad ingots before my messenger's eyes and said "if you would want to take these, take these; if you wouldn't want to take these, leave my sight!*

**What do you take me for, that you treat somebody like me with such contempt?**
`what 2sg.FOR see in 1sg.FOR that 2sg.FOR treat someone like 1sg.FOR with SUPR-hatred QP`
Ike talah lápr te nata, bawa talah mikár arazil jo nata are dafensísan sho?

*What do you see in me, that you treat someone like me with the utmost hatred?*

**I have sent as messengers gentlemen like ourselves to collect the bag with my money (deposited with you) but you have treated me with contempt by sending them back to me empty-handed several times, and that through enemy territory.**
`1sg.FOR send-PST sir~ like 1pl.INCL as messenger DAT collect bag GEN money GEN 1sg.FOR from 2sg.FOR but 2sg.FOR CONT>hold-PST<$ hatred DAT 1sg.FOR INS 3pl.FOR PV-SEM>return<$ toward 1sg.FOR paw~ empty and all through land GEN enemy`

*I sent gentlepersons like us as messengers for collecting my bag of money from you, but you repeatedly held hatred for me by repeatedly returning them to me with empty paws, and all of this through an enemy's land.*

**Is there anyone among the merchants who trade with Tilmun who has treated me in this way?**
`there_be anyone merchant which work DAT Tilmun which treat-PST ACC 1sg.FOR like this PQ`
Feza asona payannyuha zah wagaser laki Tilmun zah mikáran pá nata jo ila kel?

*There's no other merchant that works for Tilmun that treated me like this, isn't there?*

**You alone treat my messenger with contempt!**
`2sg.FOR~ CONT>hold<$ hatred DAT messenger GEN 1sg.FOR EXCL`
Tau-talah anasuron fensísa laki netasona to nata au!

*You, and you alone, are holding hatred for my messenger!*

**On account of that one (trifling) mina of silver which I owe you, you feel free to speak in such a way, while I have given to the palace on your behalf 1,080 pounds of copper, and Šumi-abum has likewise given 1,080 pounds of copper, apart from what we both have had written on a sealed tablet to be kept in the temple of Shamash.**
`for that one-flat coin GEN silver which 1sg.FOR owe 2sg.FOR 2sg.FOR have claw~ DAT speak like this yet 1sg.FOR give-PST ACC palace in footpad GEN 2sg.FOR five thousand-thing pound GEN copper and Šumi-abum also give-PST five thousand-thing pound GEN copper, other_than that PV-write-PST DAT 1pl.EXCL on hidden tablet DAT keep LOC temple GEN Leshnet`
Tah ibi atamis payang to firil zah nata culafir talah, tau ban tala-tala laki mayar jo ibi, mau nata nyukran pá gundaráskalí te 

*As for that singular silver coin which I owe you, you have the claws to speak like this, yet in your soles I gave the palace 1,080 pounds of copper, and Šumi-abum also gave 1,080 pounds of copper, other than what's been written for us on a private tablet to keep at the temple to Leshnet.*

**How have you treated me for that copper?**
`2sg.PST treat how ACC 1sg.FOR DAT that copper QP`

*How'd you treat me for this copper?*

**You have withheld my money bag from me in enemy territory; it is now up to you to restore (my money) to me in full.**
`2sg.FOR away-hold-PST bag GEN money GEN 1sg.FOR in land GEN enemy now 2sg.FOR should restore-IMP all that DAT 1sg.FOR`

*You withheld my money bag in an enemy's land; now you ought to restore all of it to me.*

**Take cognizance that (from now on) I will not accept here any copper from you that is not of fine quality.**

*You ought to understand that forevermore I will not accept a single piece of copper from you if it isn't high-quality.*

**I shall (from now on) select and take the ingots individually in my own yard, and I shall exercise against you my right of rejection because you have treated me with contempt.**
`for eternity 1sg.FOR choose-IMP and take-IMP ingot~ one and one from land GEN 1sg.FOR and 1sg.FOR use-IMP right GEN rejection ACC 2sg.FOR because 2sg.FOR CONT>hold-PST<$ hatred DAT 1sg.FOR`

*Forevermore I shall choose and take ingots one by one from my own land, and I shall use my right to rejection on you because you have been holding hatred for me.*
