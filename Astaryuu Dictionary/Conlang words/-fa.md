# Rokadong
## Pronunciation
(*Pahang*) IPA: [-fɐ]
!!{small}The tone of the affix depends on the stress of the word it is on.!!

## Suffix
!!{large}**** • _-fa_!!
### Origin

### Definition
1. *Turns words, usually nouns or verbs, into diminutive nouns.*
	Examples:
		[[salar]] "to press" → [[salafa]] "button"
		[[ashora]] "today" → [[ashofa]] "now"
		[[sela]] "star" → [[selafa]] "soul"
