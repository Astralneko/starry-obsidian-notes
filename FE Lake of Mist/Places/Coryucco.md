#fe-lake-of-mist #coryucco
The **Republic of Coryucco** is a small nation to the south of [[Darralucia]]. As the name implies, it styles itself as the strongest democracy in the known world, though fellow historically democratic states [[Newbeglia]] and [[Isola della Nebbia]] joke that this is only because it has a small population even for its small size. It hasn't even been democratic for particularly long, as it used a similar political structure to [[Darralucia]] and [[Newbeglia]] several generations past.
## Geography
Most of Coryucco faces away from the Mercanti Sea, but land routes and routes through [[Darralucia]] mean it still sees regular trade.

The largest towns are Markesh and Kenitta, both on or near the ocean. However, the Humpback Road, a land-based trade route, includes many towns on its sides, some of which are considered Coryuccan, while others have their own affiliations. The Humpback Road ends in Treplis City, the heart of the [[Treplis|Treplian Caliphate]] south of [[Isola della Nebbia]], where it is continued in spirit as a sea route until reaching Sandrolia, [[Newbeglia]].
## Demographics
Coryucco is the only nation aside from Darralucia whose population is mostly laguz.
